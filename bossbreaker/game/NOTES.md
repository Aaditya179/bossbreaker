# Bossbreaker prototype

## Play and test

Run `npm run -s serve` and open http://localhost:4173. Drag cards horizontally to reorder the hand; the selected cards execute in hand order. Tap a card to select it for play, up to capacity. Tap its orange × to mark it for discard without consuming capacity. Discard & Draw replaces both selected and discard-marked cards for one battle-wide discard. Tap a selected combo card then an enemy to change its target. A dead target falls back to the first living enemy. Area attacks affect all living enemies.

Play Combo resolves a nonempty selection automatically; Pass Turn requires a second tap to confirm. Inputs are locked while actions and enemy responses resolve. Six cards are drawn at each planning turn; unplayed and executed cards enter the discard pile and are reshuffled into the draw pile as needed. Newly discarded cards can be drawn only when the remaining draw pile and earlier discards cannot satisfy a draw. Each physical copy has a stable ID. No manual movement, aiming or energy is required.

Combo capacity starts at 5 and increases by 1 after each floor boss, to 8. Cards consume 1–3 units, with level-three evolution adding one. The six-card hand does not grow with capacity. Read enemy intents and statuses before executing. Guard lasts through the enemy response, Dodge evades one hit, and Riposte counters one blocked or evaded hit. Dash followed by Slash hits all enemies; Echo repeats the next eligible card. Use the Deck and Status buttons for details. Pause offers reduced-motion, fixed-step fast-combat, shake, optional card-selection haptics and sound-volume settings. Mouse and touch are supported; keyboard and gamepad controls are optional.

The Deck viewer lists owned copies, levels, sizes, families, piles and relics. Between encounters, it can sell a specific copy for gold; rare or level-three sales require confirmation. At least eight cards, an attack and a Guard must remain. The five-family limit counts Sword, Defense, Counter, Arcane, Elemental (Fire and Frost), and Storm; selling the last copy frees a family slot. The map shop sells weapons and armor for gold; equipment can be equipped or sold between rooms. Level-three upgrades add size and an effect, and previews disclose both. Encounters grant 12 gold (18 cursed, 25 boss); normal fights offer a card, an eligible copy upgrade or recovery. Normal reward rerolls cost 15 gold, increasing by 15 on each reroll for that screen. Tap a reward twice to confirm. Bosses guarantee capacity and offer one of three cards: Legendary Sovereign Strike, Mythical Aegis Veil, or Cursed Blood Blade. The cursed card costs health when played. Campfires and treasure provide additional choices or rewards.

## Combat rules

Consecutive elements react in addition to their normal effects: Fire → Frost deals 10 area damage and Weakens; Frost → Fire deals 14 extra target damage; Fire + Storm deals 8 area damage; Frost + Storm deals 10 extra damage and Armor Break; Fire + Fire adds Burn; Frost + Frost adds Chill; Storm + Storm deals 8 area damage. Non-elemental cards break the chain. The Combos button shows discovered recipes; discoveries persist locally between runs. `cheat reset-combos` resets them. Direct attacks have a 10% chance to deal 1.5× direct damage; reaction damage, status ticks and counters cannot crit. Overload has a 15% chance to add 5 area damage. Phantom Edge has a 12% chance to repeat a sword attack at half strength without chaining. These rolls are made by the combat rules using the seeded game RNG and recorded in battle's random log.

Statuses tick after the enemy response. Poison deals 2 per stack and Burn 3 per stack; each caps at 5 stacks for two ticks. Chill subtracts 4 damage per stack, Weaken subtracts 3, and Armor Break adds 3 incoming damage; each caps at 2 stacks for two turns. Paralysis reduces next-turn capacity by 1 and Silence prevents elemental cards from being queued; each lasts one turn and caps at one stack. Reapplying a status refreshes its duration. Statuses applied by enemies persist into the next planning turn. The Status viewer describes these rules in game.

The Dread Knight applies Armor Break, the Lich can Silence, and the Abyssal Overlord inflicts Burn and Paralysis on telegraphed attacks. The Sentinel armor set grants +4 Guard at two pieces and +6 Riposte at three. Ember grants +3 Fire at two and +3 sword damage at three. A Runed Sword adds 4 sword damage; an Ash Wand adds 3 elemental damage.

## Validation and development

`npm test` runs deterministic combat and deck tests plus seeded encounter simulations. With the local server running, `npm run -s test:interactions` exercises actual desktop browser interactions and `npm run -s test:mobile` exercises touch layouts at 320, 375 and 430 pixels. `npm run -s build` builds the game; `cheat gold <amount>` sets run gold for testing; `npm run -s smoke -- --seconds 45` checks runtime startup. The simulation compares representative sword, elemental, defense, heavy, evolved and relic builds against isolated normal and boss encounters; it is a tuning aid, not a complete-run win-rate estimate.

`run.ts` owns persistent copies, capacity, rewards and run progression. `equipment.ts` defines shop transactions and equipped bonuses; `ui/cards.ts` renders shared card visuals. `ui/feedback.ts` consumes resolved combat events for banners, floating numbers and the actual damage total; it does not apply rules. `arena.ts` manages combat, piles, targets and the state machine. `status.ts`, `elements.ts` and `combos.ts` hold shared status, reaction and discovery rules. The Phaser scenes and `ui/arenaView.ts` render and animate those decisions. `art.ts` contains the existing pixel art and synthesized effects.

## Known limits

The portrait interface fits tested viewport sizes, but secondary card text is dense at 320px. Card foil, reward reveals and equipment visuals are intentionally lightweight rather than full 3D or paper-doll animation. Fast-forward uses additional fixed combat steps per rendered frame; reduced motion limits presentation movement. Physical gamepad devices, real phones, vibration support and Reddit Devvit integration have not been verified. Balancing remains a prototype; isolated simulations cannot establish complete-run fairness or cover every build. Future three-element reactions are not implemented. The shop has a small fixed equipment catalog; high-rarity reveals and equipment animations are simplified.
